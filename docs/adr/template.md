# ADR NNNN — <short title in the imperative>

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** <names>

## Context

What forces are at play? What constraints are real (team size, time budget, self-hosting,
source limits)? Write this so someone who joins in a year understands the situation without
having been there. No solution talk yet.

## Decision

What we are doing, stated plainly and in the present tense.

## Rationale

Why this option beat the others. Be concrete — "cheaper" and "simpler" are not reasons
until you say cheaper in what and simpler for whom.

## Alternatives considered

Each one, and the specific reason it lost. An ADR with no rejected alternatives is a note,
not a decision.

## Consequences

What this costs us, what it forecloses, and what new obligations it creates. Include the
bad parts — this section is what makes the ADR worth reading later.
